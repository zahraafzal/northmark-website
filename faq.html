📦 Orders

Q: How do I place an order?
Simply browse our products, select your size and color, and click "Add to Bag". When ready, go to your cart and proceed to checkout. You can checkout as a guest or create an account for faster future purchases.
Q: Can I modify or cancel my order?
Once an order is placed, we process it quickly to ensure fast shipping. Please contact us immediately at support@northmark.com if you need to make changes. If your order hasn't shipped yet, we'll do our best to help.
Q: Do you offer gift cards?
Yes! Gift cards are available in denominations of $25, $50, $100, and $200. They're delivered via email and never expire.

🚚 Shipping

Q: How much does shipping cost?
  • US: $5.99 standard (5-7 days), FREE on orders over $150
  • Canada: $9.99 standard (7-10 days)
  • International: $14.99+ (10-14 days)
Express shipping options available at checkout.
Q: How long will my order take to arrive?
Orders are processed within 1-2 business days. Shipping times:
  • US Standard: 5-7 business days
  • US Express: 2-3 business days
  • International: 10-14 business days
Q: Do you ship internationally?
Yes! We ship worldwide to over 50 countries. International customers are responsible for customs duties and taxes.
Q: How can I track my order?
You'll receive a tracking number via email once your order ships. You can also track your order on our Track Order page using your order number and email.

↩️ Returns & Exchanges

Q: What is your return policy?
We offer easy returns within 14 days of delivery. Items must be unworn, unwashed, with original tags attached. See our full Return Policy for details.
Q: Do you offer exchanges?
We don't offer direct exchanges at this time. Simply return your item for a refund and place a new order for the size/color you want.
Q: How long does a refund take?
Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days of receiving your return. It may take an additional 3-5 days for the refund to appear in your account depending on your bank.

📏 Sizing & Fit

Q: How do I find my size?
Check our Size Guide on each product page. We provide detailed measurements for chest, waist, and length. Our fit is true to size. If between sizes, we recommend sizing up.
Q: What if the item doesn't fit?
No problem! Return it within 14 days for a full refund. Make sure it's unworn with tags attached.
Q: Are your products true to size?
Yes, our products run true to size. However, some styles may have a relaxed or oversized fit - this will be noted in the product description.

💳 Payments

Q: What payment methods do you accept?
We accept:
  • All major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express)
  • PayPal
  • Apple Pay
  • Google Pay
Q: Is it safe to use my credit card?
Absolutely! We use industry-standard SSL encryption to protect your payment information. Your card details are never stored on our servers - they go directly to our secure payment processor.
Q: Do you offer payment plans?
Not at this time. Full payment is required at checkout.

👕 Products

Q: What are your products made of?
We use premium heavyweight cotton (280-320 GSM) for t-shirts and heavyweight fleece (400+ GSM) for hoodies and crewnecks. Exact fabric content is listed on each product page.
Q: How do I care for my NORTHMARK items?
  • Machine wash cold with like colors
  • Tumble dry low or hang dry
  • Do not bleach
  • Iron inside out if needed
Q: When will sold-out items be restocked?
Restocks vary by item. Sign up for our newsletter or enable "back in stock" notifications on the product page to be notified when items return.

👤 Account

Q: Do I need an account to order?
No, you can checkout as a guest. However, creating an account lets you track orders, save addresses, and checkout faster in the future.
Q: How do I reset my password?
Click "Forgot Password?" on the login page and enter your email. You'll receive a password reset link within minutes.
Q: How do I update my email or address?
Log into your account and go to Account Settings. You can update all your information there.
