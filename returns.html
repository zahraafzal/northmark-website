Return Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

14-Day Return Window
We offer easy returns within 14 days of delivery. Your satisfaction is our priority.

1. Return Eligibility

To be eligible for a return, your item must be:

  • Unworn, unwashed, and in original condition
  • With all original tags attached
  • Returned within 14 days of delivery
  • In original packaging (if possible)

2. Non-Returnable Items

The following items cannot be returned:

  • Final sale items (marked as such)
  • Items marked as non-returnable
  • Items damaged after delivery
  • Items without original tags

3. How to Return

Step 1: Email us at returns@northmark.com with your order number

Step 2: Pack your item securely in original packaging

Step 3: Ship to our return address (provided via email)

Step 4: We'll process your refund within 5-7 business days

4. Return Shipping

Who Pays Return Shipping?

  • Customer: Standard returns - you pay return shipping
  • We Pay: Defective items or wrong items sent

5. Refund Method

Refunds are issued to your original payment method:

  • Credit/Debit Card: 5-7 business days
  • PayPal: 3-5 business days
  • Bank Transfer: 7-10 business days

6. Exchanges

We currently do not offer direct exchanges. If you need a different size or color:

  • Return your original item for a refund
  • Place a new order for the item you want

7. Defective or Damaged Items

If you received a defective or damaged item:

  • Contact us within 48 hours of delivery
  • Send photos of the defect/damage
  • We'll provide a prepaid return label
  • Full refund or replacement will be issued
