Return Policy

14-Day Return Window

Eligible items can be returned within 14 days of delivery, subject to the conditions below.

01

Return Eligibility

To qualify for a return, your item must meet all of the following conditions:

  • Unworn, unwashed, and in its original condition.
  • All original tags must remain attached.
  • The return must be requested within 14 days of delivery.
  • Original packaging should be included where possible.
02

Non-Returnable Items

For the following items, returns are not accepted under this policy:

  • Final sale items clearly marked as final sale.
  • Items specifically marked as non-returnable.
  • Items damaged after delivery.
  • Items returned without their original tags.
03

How to Return

Follow these four steps to start your return:

01

Contact Our Team

Email returns@northmark.com with your order number.

02

Pack Your Item

Securely pack the item, including its original tags.

03

Ship Your Return

Use the return address provided by our team via email.

04

Refund Processing

We aim to process eligible refunds within 5–7 business days.

04

Return Shipping

Return shipping costs are handled as follows:

  • Standard returns: The customer pays the return shipping cost.
  • Wrong or defective items: NORTHMARK covers return shipping.
05

Refund Method & Timing

Refunds are issued to the original payment method. Estimated processing times are:

  • Credit or debit card: 5–7 business days.
  • PayPal: 3–5 business days.
  • Bank transfer: 7–10 business days.

Please Note

The timeframes above are estimates. Your bank or payment provider may require additional time for the refund to appear.

06

Exchanges

Direct exchanges are currently unavailable. If you need a different size or colour:

  • Request a return for your original eligible item.
  • Place a new order for the size or colour you prefer.
    • 07

    Defective or Damaged Items

    If your order arrives damaged, defective, or incorrect, please:

    • Contact us within 48 hours of delivery.
    • Include clear photos showing the issue.
    • Wait for our team to provide return instructions and a prepaid return label where applicable.
    • We will arrange an eligible full refund or replacement.
    08

    Need Help?

    Our team is here to help with your return questions.
